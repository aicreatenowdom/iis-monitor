# ServerSight Pro support

For setup, monitoring, trial, purchase or activation help, contact **AI Creations Now Software Development** at [info@aicreatenow.com](mailto:info@aicreatenow.com) or **1-866-315-4750**.

Current downloads, requirements and license information are available at **[ServerSightPro.com](https://serversightpro.com/)**.

## Before contacting support

Include the product name, application version, Windows version, IIS environment, a short description of the issue and the steps that reproduce it. Mention any compatibility guidance or missing-component messages shown by the application.

For a monitoring question, include:

- The affected selected site and session interval, including the time zone.
- The specific view and metric you are comparing.
- Whether Cloudflare or another CDN/cache is involved.
- Relevant error text and a reviewed screenshot or small diagnostic excerpt.

For purchase or key-delivery questions, include the checkout email address in your **private** message to support. Payment alone does not activate the software; enter the delivered 10-digit key in the application. For missing or damaged trial and registration records, contact support instead of deleting licensing state.

Do not post passwords, API tokens, full license keys, checkout details or unredacted logs in public issues. Reports can include client IP addresses, query strings, server names and other sensitive information. Share only the information needed for the case, privately with support when necessary.
